After all this ended, I understood that things that have intrinsic value for me, i.e. things that made me better HERE AND NOW, should never be confused with (and traded for) things with merely instrumental value that can make me better POTENTIALLY. I understood why I shouldn't date girls I don't love. I wasn't going to walk on false paths where the true path existed... probably.

Later I repeated the same mistake a million times. But it was different. At least I knew it was a mistake. And I knew that I was going to be disappointed.
