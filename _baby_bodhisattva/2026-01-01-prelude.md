In the worse days it still feels like before.

Sometimes when someone yells at me, or when I have to go to the office of the municipality or something I still feel lost, I remember that I will die and I start being angry at stuff for no reason (e.g. that the president is a jerk). 

In the worst days I still feel the urge to escape, to that one place from which I cannot come back (or at least some cheaper alternative).

But I am often better. I often remember that I WAS on the other shore. That I already decided that the spiritual side of life matters more to me than everything else.

It took me some time to do that, because it meant nullifying all my achievements, it meant admitting I am nothing (but in both a  good and a bad way). It meant not being able to participate in most conversations that most people are having, but it is worth it.
